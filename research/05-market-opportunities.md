**What this means for him:** the money is at the cheap end. Cars under $15k are the scarcest band (34 days' supply against 49 overall), their share of the market has nearly halved since 2019, and high gas prices are pushing compact-car values up while SUVs, pickups and luxury cars soften. ### 1.3 How many cars one person can handle, and the legal cap

- **Maryland:** a person who offers to sell **3 or more** vehicles in any 12 months is presumed to be a dealer (DOCUMENTED – Transportation §15-101, https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2026RS/Statute_Web/gtr/15-101.pdf; text read through a search extract). In practice that means **2 cars a year unlicensed**.
- **Virginia:** a dealer is someone who sells or offers **5 or more** in 12 consecutive months (DOCUMENTED – 24VAC22-20 and DMV dealer definition, https://transactions-d.dmv.virginia.gov/documents/dealer_def.pdf). In practice that means **4 cars a year unlicensed**.
- **DC:** no threshold confirmed in this pass.
- Physical capacity for one person with a licence: 3–6 cars a month is realistic for sub-$10k cars with outsourced detailing and mechanics (UNTESTED GUESS). Vendor marketing claims "2–3 flips a month can replace a salary" (REPORTED, Flipify), which ignores the legal cap.
- **Getting licensed:** Virginia needs an established place of business that passes zoning, a sign with 6-inch letters, a bond, and a salesperson licence for the dealer-operator (DOCUMENTED – https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/businesses/dealers/car-truck-license). Maryland needs a bond sized to projected volume, a site inspection and an orientation (DOCUMENTED – https://mva.maryland.gov/your-mva-guide/businesses/vehicle-dealers-title-services/business-licenses-vehicle-dealers/vehicle-dealer). Current bond amounts were not confirmed (the old MD code set $15,000 for 1–250 cars). A licence also opens dealer-only auctions (Manheim, ADESA), which changes the sourcing maths.

**Conclusion:** unlicensed flipping in the DMV is a side income of $2,000–$6,000 a year (UNTESTED GUESS). Replacing an income from cars means a licence or a job buying for a licensed dealer.

### 1.4 Wholesale-to-retail and private-to-dealer spreads

- No source publishes a single 2026 wholesale-to-retail spread. What exists: retail listings rose about 6% YoY over summer 2026 while the wholesale index rose about 2%, which suggests the gap widened (REPORTED – derived from Cox series via secondary coverage). Dealer gross per car ($1,400–$2,100, above) is the practical measure of the spread after reconditioning.
- Trade-in vs private-party sale gap: $1,000–$4,000 typical; one 2026 analysis of 1,200 matched cars found $2,418 (REPORTED – CarCheckerVIN, https://www.carcheckervin.com/blog/trade-in-vs-private-sale; Bumper). That gap is what a private buyer-reseller captures: buy near trade-in value from people who want a quick sale, sell near private value.

### 4.2 Where the gap is

1. **Price gap between $47 and $1,695 a month.** Consumer-grade alert apps sit at $28–$352. Dealer acquisition systems start at about $500–$1,695. Small independent dealers, the "buy here, pay here" lots and the one-man wholesalers in the DMV get nothing local and curated in the $100–$300 range (UNTESTED GUESS on demand; the price gap itself is DOCUMENTED from the pricing pages above).
2. **Sold-price history.** None of the alert apps advertise "this car type sold in N days at $X locally". CraigsCars already keeps price history, so it can.
3. **A human eye.** A daily "five best DMV cars under $8k, with notes from a man who has read ten thousand loan files" is a product no algorithm-only app offers.

A brand-new channel will not reach the old bar in ten weeks, so plan on the 8,000-hour bar: typically 9–18 months for a niche channel that posts every week (UNTESTED GUESS).

### 6.2 Crowded vs open

- **Crowded:** generic "I flipped a car for $X profit", Copart rebuilds (Goonzquad-sized incumbents), "dealer secrets" (CarEdge and many former salespeople), generic personal-finance advice.
- **Open:** **"The loan officer's view"**: a man who read decades of real files telling true (anonymised) stories of how people got into and out of debt, then tying each story to a car or house decision. Storytelling-led finance does very well (Caleb Hammer at 4M+), and almost no loan-officer channel uses storytelling. Second opening: **data-led local car deal shows**: "The five best cars under $8,000 in DC this week, and the three traps", using CraigsCars data no other creator has. Older, credible presenters are under-represented and trusted by older viewers, who are also the private sellers of well-kept Camrys (UNTESTED GUESS).

### 6.3 How the channel and the flipping feed each other

1. Every car bought is an episode: find it in the tool, inspect, negotiate, fix, sell, reveal the profit. That gives a steady supply of content at no extra cost.
2. Viewers become sellers ("my mother's car, would you look at it?"), which brings estate and elderly-owner cars below market. This is the best sourcing channel in the business.
3. Viewers become buyers of his cars and clients of the advisor service and alert subscription.
4. Mortgage stories carry the higher finance RPM; car episodes carry the audience. Mixing them suits YouTube's interest-matching.
5. The channel doubles as public proof of honesty, which matters for a private seller in a market where one 2026 source claims 34% of Facebook Marketplace ads are scams (REPORTED, weak source).

## 7. Things that should change the plan

1. **The unlicensed legal cap (MD: about 2 a year; VA: about 4)** means car flipping alone cannot replace an income without a dealer licence. Decide early whether a licence and a place of business are acceptable. If not, the engine's value is mainly in #3, #4 and content, not resale.
2. **Mortgage market at 7.11%** with a possible December rate hike: income from mortgage-related work (#1, #5) will be lumpy. Apply widely and early; a rate fall in 2027 would lift both quickly.
3. **YouTube bar doubles on 1 Feb 2027.** Start filming now anyway, but budget no channel income in 2027's first half.
4. **The engine's distinct asset is sold-price proxy history.** Competitors sell speed; CraigsCars can sell "what it actually sells for here, and how fast". Build that view before anything else.
5. **Under-$15k is the sweet spot:** scarcest supply, rising compact values, buyers pushed to cash by 11–19% loan rates. Point the engine's defaults there.
